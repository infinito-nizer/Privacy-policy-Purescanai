PureScan AI Privacy Policy

How PureScan AI processes food label scans, preferences, authentication, and subscription records responsibly.

Application: PureScan AI (com.aistudio.purescan.toxndetect)
Effective Date: November 14, 2026
Version: 1.2

1. Commitment to Privacy & Zero Data Selling

Your privacy is our foremost priority. PureScan AI strictly never sells, rents, monetizes, or brokers your personal information, dietary preferences, food scan history, or uploaded photographs to third-party data aggregators, advertising networks, or marketing brokers.

Summary: PureScan AI processes only the minimum data needed to analyze food ingredients, maintain your personal allergy/toxin filters, manage your 24-hour free trial, and verify optional 1-month PureScan AI Pro subscriptions.

2. Information We Collect & Process

  • Food Label, Packaging & Meal Photographs: Camera captures and gallery images you choose to scan are processed ephemerally via Firebase AI Logic and Google Gemini exclusively to extract ingredient text, detect banned chemical additives or allergens, and compute Clean Scores. Optional local thumbnails may be cached on your device for your scan history.
  • Safety Shield Preferences & Scan History: Your selected sensitivity filters (such as artificial dyes, endocrine disruptors, seed oils, preservatives, gluten, dairy, soy, or PFAS packaging warnings) and scan reports are stored locally on your device using an Android Room database and, when signed in with Google, synchronized to your private Cloud Firestore workspace (/users/{userId}/plans and /users/{userId}/scans).
  • Google Authentication Profile: If you sign in with Google via Android Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication, we receive your Firebase User ID (UID), display name, email address, and profile photo URL solely for account authentication and cross-device cloud sync. We never access or store your Google password. Guest sessions remain local to your device.
  • Subscription & Verified Payment Identifiers: Paid 1-month PureScan AI Pro subscriptions are processed externally over encrypted HTTPS connections by PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (Razorpay in India; Dodo Payments in the USA and globally). PureScan AI never collects, processes, or stores credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, bank account credentials, or UPI PINs. We store only your subscription tier, trial/active validity timestamps, gateway identifier, and verified payment/session reference IDs (/users/{userId}/subscription/current).

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA): Requested strictly when you use live food label or dish scanning. Camera frames are never captured or transmitted in the background. You can revoke camera permission at any time in your Android system settings.
  • Photo Selection (Android Photo Picker): Choosing an image from your gallery uses Android's zero-permission Photo Picker so PureScan AI can only read the single image you explicitly select—never your full media library.
  • Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required for cloud AI ingredient analysis, Firebase App Check attestation, Google Sign-In, Cloud Firestore sync, and hosted checkout sessions.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

PureScan AI relies on trusted infrastructure providers strictly to deliver core app functionality:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, Firebase AI Logic): Provides zero-trust per-user data isolation, app attestation, and Gemini multimodal ingredient analysis.
  • Razorpay & Dodo Payments: Process regional and global 1-month subscription payments and return server-verifiable transaction status so Pro access is activated securely for your Google account.

5. Security & Zero-Trust Data Isolation

All network communications use TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit. Cloud Firestore records are strictly protected by zero-trust security rules ensuring that only the authenticated owner (request.auth.uid == userId) can read or write their own safety plans, scan reports, and subscription status. Unauthenticated or guest callers cannot read or modify another user's cloud records, nor purchase subscriptions without Google authentication.

6. Data Retention & Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with the EU GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP):

  • Access & Portability: You may inspect your scan history, safety profile, and subscription status directly within the app.
  • Local & Cloud Deletion: You can delete individual scans or clear scan history directly inside PureScan AI, revoke Google access via your Google Account security settings, or contact us by email to request permanent deletion of your Firestore account data.
  • No Targeted Advertising: PureScan AI contains zero third-party ad SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.

7. Children's Privacy (Age 13+)

PureScan AI is intended strictly for individuals aged 13 years or older (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, such as 16 under GDPR where required). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 in compliance with COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policies. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us immediately so we can delete those records.

8. Educational & Non-Medical Disclaimer

PureScan AI is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Clean Scores (0–100), additive alerts, and EU/FDA status indicators are informational only and may contain OCR or visual recognition inaccuracies. Always verify physical product packaging and consult a licensed physician or allergist for medical or severe allergy decisions.
